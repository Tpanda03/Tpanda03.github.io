About

I'm a coterminal MS student at Illinois Institute of Technology studying Robotics and Autonomous Systems — expected to graduate December 2026. My undergrad was in Electrical & Computer Engineering + Computer Science.

My work lives at the intersection of embedded systems, sensor fusion, and safety-critical software. I care about systems that have to work correctly in the real world — on actual hardware, under real constraints.

Most recently I was at Intel doing RAS validation on Granite Rapids Xeon silicon. Outside of work, I contribute to EcoCAR as a sensor fusion engineer and have a published paper from my capstone project PULSE at IEEE EIT 2025.

My thesis work focuses on an uncertainty-aware safety controller for ACC/LCC grounded in the ISO 21448 SOTIF standard — bringing conformal prediction and formal safety frameworks to real autonomous vehicles.
